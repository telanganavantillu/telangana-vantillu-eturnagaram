Our Story

Vantillu means “our homes”

In the villages of Telangana, the kitchen is the heart of the home — a firewood chulha, a well-seasoned clay pot, and recipes passed from mother to daughter. We cook exactly that way.

Our Eturnagaram branch sits at Y Junction, Akulavari Ghanpur, on the road to the historic Eturnagaram forests. Whether you're a local family, a traveller to the Sammakka–Saralamma country, or simply hungry for real Telangana food, you'll be served like a guest at home.

  • Firewood & clay-pot cooking
  • Hand-pounded, house-made masalas
  • Millets & village favorites — jonna, sajja, ragi
  • Fresh country chicken & river fish
